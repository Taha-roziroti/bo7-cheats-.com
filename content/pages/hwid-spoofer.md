## What does an HWID spoofer do?

HWID stands for Hardware Identification.

In simple terms, hardware identifiers are values associated with components of a computer. An HWID spoofer is software designed to change or mask selected hardware identifiers.

The ZADEYO product description specifically references identifiers such as:

- Disk serial numbers
- MAC addresses
- GPU IDs
- Other related hardware identifiers

The idea is pretty straightforward. You're looking at a product focused on hardware identification rather than a package of gameplay features like ESP or aimbot.
